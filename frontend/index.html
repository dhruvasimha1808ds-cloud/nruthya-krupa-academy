Ten years of Nruthya Krupa Kala Shale

For ten years, Nruthya Krupa Kala Shale has stood in Mandya as a place where Bharatanatyam is taught not as performance alone, but as a path of artistic excellence, self-discovery, and spiritual growth.

Heritage of the Art Form

Bharatanatyam — the mother of classical dance

Bharatanatyam is regarded as the oldest of India's classical dance forms, rooted in the temples of Tamil Nadu over two thousand years ago, and traced to the Natya Shastra.

  • Origin
    Attributed to Lord Shiva as Nataraja, and revealed by Lord Brahma to the sage Bharata through the Natya Shastra.
  • Aramandi
    The half-seated stance that grounds the entire vocabulary of Bharatanatyam movement.
  • Mudras
    Precise hand gestures through which the dancer speaks — of gods, of nature, of emotion.
  • Attire
    The pleated silk saree, temple jewelry, ghungroo, and expressive eye makeup — worn as an offering, not a costume.
